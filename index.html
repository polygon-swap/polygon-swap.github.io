What is it

Swapping tokens on Polygon PoS

One chain, one gas token

A Polygon swap exchanges one token for another on Polygon PoS, a proof-of-stake network connected to Ethereum. Its native coin is POL, which replaced MATIC as the gas and staking token, so every transaction on the chain is paid for in POL.

What people trade here

Typical pairs are POL against USDC or USDT, stablecoin to stablecoin, and POL against bridged ETH. A DEX or aggregator reads the available pools, finds a route and shows the expected output before you sign anything.

How to swap

How to swap on Polygon

Quote first, sign second

The interface shows the route, the expected amount out, price impact and your slippage tolerance. Nothing happens on-chain until you confirm in your wallet.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and switch it to the Polygon network.
  2. Choose the token to sell and the token to receive, then enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, route and estimated network fee in POL.
  4. Approve the sell token once if it is an ERC-20 and this is its first swap from this wallet.
  5. Sign the swap and wait for it to confirm; the new tokens arrive in your wallet.
Fees & gas

What a Polygon swap costs

Network fee in POL

Gas on Polygon PoS is paid in POL and is generally small compared with Ethereum mainnet, though it still varies with network load. Read how the chain works in the Polygon docs.

Trading cost

The quote already includes pool fees, price impact and any aggregator fee. Thin pools move the price more, so large trades in small tokens cost more than the headline gas suggests.

Keep some POL

If your whole balance is in USDC or USDT you cannot pay gas. Hold a little POL in the same wallet so you can approve and sign.

Security

Staying safe on Polygon

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction. This interface routes and quotes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Confirm the token contract address; copycat tokens with real-looking names are common on any chain.
  • Make sure your wallet is on Polygon and not another network.
  • Approve only the amount you need and revoke allowances you no longer use.
  • Check price impact and slippage, especially on low-liquidity tokens.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Polygon Swap FAQ

What is Polygon Swap used for?

It is a non-custodial interface for swapping tokens on Polygon PoS, such as POL, USDC and USDT. You connect your own wallet, review a quote and sign the transaction yourself.

Is POL the same as MATIC?

POL is the successor to MATIC and is now the native gas and staking token of Polygon PoS. Wallets and explorers may still show MATIC in older labels, but gas is paid in POL.

Which token do I need to pay gas on Polygon?

You pay gas in POL for every transaction, including approvals and swaps. Keep a small POL balance even if most of your funds are in stablecoins.

Why are there two versions of USDC on Polygon?

Native USDC is issued directly on the network, while USDC.e is an older version that arrived through a bridge. They are different contracts, so confirm which one you are selling or receiving.

Do I need to approve a token before swapping?

Yes for ERC-20 tokens like USDC and USDT, because the router needs an allowance to move them. The approval is a separate transaction that costs a little gas. Swapping native POL needs no approval.

Why did I receive less than the quote?

The final amount can differ if the price moves before your transaction confirms. Your slippage tolerance sets the limit, and the swap reverts if the result would fall below it.
